## Intent

**Problem:** Playtest: on-screen text (HUD, pause/debug menu, popups, damage numbers, controls hint) is pixelated and hard to read. The canvas is authored on the 640x400 grid with legacy `Text` at 7-13 px plus a 1-reference-px `Outline` effect that scales into blocky offset copies.

**Approach:** Keep the world pixel-perfect (640x400 ref, 32 PPU unchanged). UI stays on a Screen Space - Overlay canvas (drawn by URP after the final blit at native resolution), re-authored for a 1280x800 reference (match 0.5) with TextMeshPro SDF text (LiberationSans, OFL) and an SDF outline + soft underlay material; HUD text >= 20 px at reference. World-anchored popups keep projecting world->screen into the overlay. All of it is generated by `SandboxBuilder`.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** builder is the source of truth (Rebuild Sandbox reproduces everything); TMP Essential Resources imported batchmode-safely (idempotent); N-player HUD placement and gamepad/keyboard menu navigation keep working; BannedApiTests green; keep uncommitted tuning edits (RAGE grace 4.5 s, Thunderbolt cooldown 1.5 s).

**Never:** change PPU or the 640x400 reference; commit; download third-party fonts.
